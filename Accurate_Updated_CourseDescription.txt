🌸 ITC Beautician Training – Professional Course Descriptions
1. Beautician Basic Course — 2 Months

A perfect foundation course for beginners entering the beauty industry.
Students learn essential skin care, hair care, grooming, and makeup fundamentals required for salon work.
Ideal for anyone starting their beautician journey.

You will learn:

Basic skin care & facials

Everyday makeup

Hair washing, drying, & basic styling

Threading & waxing introduction

Personal grooming & hygiene

2. Beautician Advance Course — 4 Months

An in-depth professional training program designed to upgrade your skills and prepare you for working confidently in salons or running your own beauty business.

You will learn:

Advanced facial treatments

Advanced hair techniques

Professional makeup basics

Nail care & extensions

Salon tools & product knowledge

3. Abroad Beautician Course — 1 Month

A fast-track course specially designed for students preparing for beauty-related work abroad.
Focuses on internationally demanded skills and techniques.

You will learn:

International-style makeup

Hair styling and care

Basic nail and lash skills

Grooming & customer handling

Job-ready beauty skills

4. Professional Makeup Course — 1 Month

A complete makeup artistry program for students who want to specialize in bridal, party, photoshoot, and event makeup.

You will learn:

Bridal makeup techniques

Party & engagement makeup

Contouring, highlighting, and skin prep

Product knowledge & shade matching

Live practice sessions

Self Makeup Variants:

15 Days — Personal grooming & self makeup

1 Week — Quick essential makeup skills

3–5 Days — Fast-track self makeup training

5. Professional Nail Art Course — 1.5 Months

A detailed course covering modern nail artistry, extensions, and salon techniques.
Perfect for students aiming to become professional nail technicians.

You will learn:

Nail extensions

Gel polish application

Nail art design & patterns

Shape correction

Salon hygiene & tool handling

Includes:

Gel Nail Training — 10 Days

6. Microblading & Microshading — 15 Days

A specialized semi-permanent makeup course focused on creating natural-looking eyebrows.

You will learn:

Eyebrow mapping

Microblading strokes

Microshading techniques

Color theory

Safety & aftercare

7. Eyelashes Extension & Lashes Lifting — 10 Days

A beginner-friendly course on lash enhancement techniques used in modern salons.

You will learn:

Classic & volume lash extension

Lash lifting procedure

Lash tinting

Product handling & safety

8. Threading Course — 5 to 7 Days

A fast, practical course teaching facial threading for salon use.

You will learn:

Eyebrow shaping

Upper lip & forehead threading

Full face threading

Hygiene & technique control

9. Manicure, Pedicure & Waxing Course — 10 Days

Focused on hand & feet care combined with professional waxing techniques.

You will learn:

Manicure steps & nail shaping

Pedicure steps & foot spa

Hand/feet massage

Waxing (arms, legs, underarms)

10. Mehandi (Henna) Course — 1 Month

A complete henna artistry course for bridal, festival, and traditional designs.

You will learn:

Basic & advanced mehendi patterns

Bridal mehendi

Cone making & paste preparation

Design symmetry & creativity

11. Combo Beautician Course (Basic + Advance + Makeup + Nail Art + Lashes Extension) — 9 Months

A comprehensive all-in-one professional career course ideal for students who want to become full-fledged beauticians.

You will learn:

Complete basic & advanced beautician training

Professional makeup

Nail art & extensions

Lashes extension & lifting

Hair chemical & styling basics

Customer service & salon readiness

Best for: Students who want a long-term career in beauty.

12. Combo Course (Basic + Advance) — 3 Months

A shorter combination program offering both basic and advanced beautician skills in one course.

You will learn:

Basic & advanced facials

Basic hair care & styling

Makeup fundamentals

Threading, waxing, manicure/pedicure

13. Special Abroad Course (Out-Going Course) — 1 Month

A focused training program specifically for students preparing to work overseas in salons or beauty centers.

You will learn:

International beauty techniques

Fast-track salon skills

Grooming & professional conduct

Makeup, hair, and basic nail skills
