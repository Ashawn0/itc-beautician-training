About Heading

About ITC Beautician Training & Service Center

Introduction

ITC Beautician Training & Service Center is a registered beauty academy in Nepal dedicated to providing professional, practical, and career-oriented beautician education. Our goal is to make quality beauty training accessible and help students develop the confidence and skills needed to build successful careers in the beauty industry.

Our Mission

To offer high-quality beautician training that blends modern techniques with practical learning, enabling students to become confident professionals ready for salon work or independent practice.

Our Vision

To become a leading beauty academy in Nepal known for producing skilled beauticians, makeup artists, hair stylists, and beauty professionals.

Our Teaching Approach

Step-by-step demonstrations

Small class sizes

Friendly environment

Focus on confidence and creativity

Why Students Choose Us
